About

This demo showcases a gesture-controlled video player using MediaPipe Hand Landmarker for real-time hand tracking. All processing happens locally in your browser—no data is uploaded to any server.

Click the "Enable Hand Control" button to activate camera access and start controlling the video with your hands. Make sure you're in a well-lit environment for best results.

Privacy: Your camera feed is processed entirely on your device using WebAssembly. No images or video frames are transmitted over the network.
