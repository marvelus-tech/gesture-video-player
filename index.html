Gesture Controls
Enable the camera. Hold a flat hand to toggle play/pause. Flick left or right to skip. Pinch (thumb to index, other fingers up), then move for seek or volume.
Open palm
Hold a flat hand toward the camera. Toggles play and pause.
Fist
Rest / cancel. Does nothing to playback. Use it to stop a pose.
Flick right
Quick swipe to the right (no pinch). Skips forward 10 seconds.
Flick left
Quick swipe to the left. Skips back 10 seconds.
Pinch, then move left or right
Thumb to index, other fingers up, then move. Left is the start of the video, right is the end.
Pinch, then move up or down
Same pinch, then move vertically. Higher is louder.
Speed control is the on-screen 1x button. Victory, I love you, and pointing up gestures do nothing in this version.
