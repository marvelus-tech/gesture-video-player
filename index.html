Gesture Controls
Enable the camera first. Hold a pose for about half a second. For seek and volume, pinch thumb to index, then move your hand — the first direction sticks.
Open palm
Face the camera with a flat open hand and hold. Plays.
Closed fist
Make a fist and hold. Pauses.
Victory (peace)
Index and middle up; thumb and the other fingers tucked. Hold. Speeds up one step.
I love you
Thumb, index, and pinky out; middle and ring folded. Hold. Slows down one step.
Pointing up
Only the index finger up, thumb tucked. Hold. Skips forward 10 seconds.
Pinch, then move left or right
Touch thumb to index, then move your hand across the frame. Left is the start of the video, right is the end. Open the pinch to stop.
Pinch, then move up or down
Same pinch, then move your hand vertically. Higher is louder. Open the pinch to stop.
Pose commands wait a beat between triggers. While you are pinching, poses are ignored.
